Correct Physics Formula Matrix

Curriculum Aligned Equations for Grade Level Scenario Based Decision Items

Purpose. This matrix presents the physics formulas corresponding to the competency areas used in the study. The formulas are editable text and may be copied directly into the manuscript or appendices.

Grade LevelCompetency AreaCorrect Formula or FormulasMeaning or Condition
Grade 7
Balanced and unbalanced forces
Fnet = ∑Fi
Balanced: ∑Fi = 0
Unbalanced: ∑Fi ≠ 0
Net force is the vector sum of all forces acting on an object.
Grade 7
Distance, displacement, speed, and velocity
vavg = dtotalΔt
vavg = ΔxΔt
Δx = xf - xi
Speed uses distance, while velocity uses displacement.
Grade 8
Acceleration
aavg = ΔvΔt
Acceleration is the change in velocity per unit time.
Grade 8
Kinetic and potential energy
K = 12mv2
Ug = mgh
K is kinetic energy; U is gravitational potential energy.
Grade 8
Work and mechanical energy
W = Fd cos θ
Emech = K + Ug
θ is the angle between the force and the displacement.
Grade 9
Inertia
∑F = 0 ⇒ v = constant
An object remains at rest or moves with constant velocity when the net force is zero.
Grade 9
Force, mass, and acceleration
∑F = ma
Newton’s second law relates net force, mass, and acceleration.
Grade 9
Action–reaction forces
FA→B = -FB→A
The two forces are equal in magnitude, opposite in direction, and act on different objects.
Grade 10
Projectile motion
x(t) = x0 + (v0 cos θ)t
y(t) = y0 + (v0 sin θ)t - 12gt2
These equations assume constant gravitational acceleration and negligible air resistance.
Grade 10
Momentum and impulse
p = mv
J = Favg Δt = Δp
Momentum is mass times velocity; impulse equals the change in momentum.
Grade 10
Collisions
∑pbefore = ∑pafter
Total momentum is conserved when the net external impulse is negligible.
Grade 11
Translational and rotational motion
vcm = ΔxcmΔt
ω = ΔθΔt
vcm = rω
The last equation applies to rolling without slipping.
Grade 11
Simple and compound machines
MA = FoutFin
τ = rF sin θ
MA is mechanical advantage, and τ is torque.
Grade 11
Physics of fluids
Fb = ρfluidgVdisplaced
Wtotal = mtotalg
Floating equilibrium: Fb = Wtotal
Buoyant force equals the total weight when the object or loaded boat floats in equilibrium.
Grade 11
Loaded boat condition
mtotal = mhull + mpassengers + mcargo
Vrequired = mtotalρfluid
Vrequired ≤ Vavailable
This is a simplified flotation condition. It is not a substitute for the boat’s rated capacity or freeboard requirement.
Grade 12
Newton’s laws of motion
∑F = ma
A nonzero net force produces acceleration.
Grade 12
Work and energy
W = Fd cos θ
Wnet = ΔK
Emech = K + Ug
These represent work, the work–energy theorem, and mechanical energy.
Grade 12
Momentum and impulse
p = mv
J = Favg Δt = Δp
Impulse equals the change in momentum.
